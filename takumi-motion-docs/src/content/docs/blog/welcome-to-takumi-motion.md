Welcome to the official blog for **Takumi-motion**!

## What is Takumi-motion?

Deterministic server-side animation engine: any React component or raw HTML string becomes a GIF, WebP, APNG, PNG sequence, sprite sheet, or still — rendered by the Rust takumi engine, byte-identical on every run.

## What to expect here

This blog is the central hub for everything related to Takumi-motion. We will be publishing:

- **Deep dives** into the architecture, design decisions, and inner workings of the project.
- **Tutorials and how-to guides** to help you get up and running quickly.
- **Release notes** and feature announcements as the project evolves.

Whether you are just discovering Takumi-motion or have been using it for a while, we are glad you are here. Stay tuned for our first official deep dive!
